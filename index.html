Molecular dynamics running in your browser, in reduced Lennard-Jones units. It starts hot and gets quenched, so you can see crystals nucleate and grow. Atoms with exactly six neighbours sit in a local hexagonal lattice and turn orange. Move your cursor through the box (or drag sideways on a phone) to push atoms around.

I'm a chemical engineer who builds and tests machine learning models for materials. Most of my research asks when a model's predictions can be trusted: how it does on chemistry it hasn't seen, how much the choice of descriptors and the handling of missing data change the answer, and whether its uncertainty estimates mean anything.

I also build the workflows around those models. That means generating candidate crystals, screening them with interatomic potentials, and checking the survivors with DFT so every result can be traced back to a calculation.

Since November 2026 I've been a Research Scientist on the Molecular Management team at Reliance Industries R&D, where I characterise and model the molecules in crude oil and refinery streams. My independent research runs alongside that. I did my MSc in Chemical Engineering at TU Delft and a dual degree in Chemical Engineering and Chemistry at BITS Pilani.

Research themes

Reliability of materials models

Out-of-distribution tests, paired evaluations across descriptor choices, and audits of the elemental property tables behind most composition models.

A Gaussian-process fit with its ±2σ band. Tap to add a training point. Left alone, it adds points where it's least sure, which is how active learning picks the next calculation.

Interatomic potentials

Fine-tuning MACE-MP-0 for adsorption on metal surfaces, uncertainty heads on learned embeddings, and pretraining for data efficiency.

Message passing on a crystal graph, the step at the heart of MACE and NequIP. Each layer, every atom gathers information from its neighbours. The readout adds up per-atom energies.

Electronic structure

DFT and GW-BSE with Quantum ESPRESSO and Yambo, from excitons in layered TMDs to relaxing generated solid-electrolyte candidates.

A momentum-indirect exciton, the subject of my MSc thesis. A photon excites an electron at Γ, phonons carry it into the Λ valley, and it stays bound to the hole left behind at Γ.

LandauAgent

An agent for Landau theory of structural phase transitions. An exact group-theory engine works out irreps, invariants and forced couplings, MACE-MP-0 supplies frozen-mode energies, and a tool-calling LLM plans and explains but never does the group theory itself. Checked on SrTiO₃ and on hybrid improper ferroelectricity in Ca₃Ti₂O₇. Built for the 2026 OSI Hackathon.

Drag the temperature below Tc and the single minimum of F(η) = (T − Tc)η² + η⁴ splits in two. The order parameter picks a side at random.

ChemMatMiss

A Python toolkit for stress-testing imputation in materials ML. It traced a failure where rare elemental support collapses, gets amplified by scaling, and pushes linear models into wild extrapolation, and it adds a label-free check that catches it. It grew into an audit of the elemental property tables behind common featurisers.

Schematic: gaps in an element-property table get imputed column by column, and the support check flags the value it can't trust.

MACE-MP-0 for CO₂ and CO adsorption

Pulled adsorption data from Catalysis-Hub over GraphQL, fine-tuned MACE-MP-0 for Cu, Ni and Pd surfaces, and ran Langevin MD on CO₂ over Cu(111).
